The Orchestra Model

The OM — Orchestra Model — is the jewel of the Craman catalogue. Recognised by musicians and collectors alike for its exceptional balance of warmth and projection, it is the shape that best expresses what Craman guitars are: instruments of precision, patience, and soul.

Juliette, pictured throughout this site, is a perfect example: a Huon pine soundboard over figured maple back and sides, with a maple fretboard and hand-inlaid rosette. Every curve was plotted by hand. Every join was fitted to within a fraction of a millimetre.

Huon Pine — A Tonewood Like No Other

Sourced from the ancient cool temperate rainforests of south-west Tasmania, Huon pine (Lagarostrobos franklinii) is one of the world's slowest-growing conifers — some trees exceeding 10,000 years of age. Its exceptionally tight, even grain and natural oil content produce a tonewood of remarkable stability and tonal clarity.

The result in a guitar soundboard is a voice that is warm yet articulate — with bell-like highs, a singing midrange, and a bass response that opens up beautifully over years of play. No two Craman guitars with Huon pine sound alike. That is the point.

Built to You

A Craman commission begins with a conversation about how you play and what you hear in your head. Scale length, nut width, body depth, bracing pattern, finish, binding — every variable is discussed, modelled, and agreed before a single piece of timber is cut.

The result is not a production instrument with your name on the label. It is an instrument that could only have been built for you.
