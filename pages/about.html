The Maker

About Craig Illman

Luthier. Educator. A lifetime spent listening to wood.

The Spark: A Lifelong Obsession with Music

I began the journey back in 1974. As a high school kid playing in local bands, music wasn't just a hobby — it was everything. But moving to the country meant that when equipment broke or needed tweaking, there was no local luthier to turn to. Out of necessity, a tinkerer was born. Those early years spent diving into setups and wiring modifications laid the foundation for a deep understanding of what makes an instrument tick.

Music became the heartbeat of our home. By the age of three, my sons were sitting on my knee, learning their first chords and inheriting a lifelong appreciation for the craft.

The Evolution: From Tinkering to Handcrafting

What started as simple modifications soon evolved into creation. Inspired by classic Fender principles, I began building electric guitars from scratch. The turning point, however, came when I built my very first acoustic guitar as a gift for my son. The experience was profound, sparking a tradition of crafting bespoke instruments as gifts for loved ones — and eventually, one-off custom guitars for musicians seeking something truly extraordinary.

Today, I continue to design and build these unique pieces right from my home workshop in Victoria, while still teaching at NCAT.

The Credential: Shaping the Next Generation of Luthiers

My professional life has always been deeply intertwined with education. While working and teaching CAD (Computer-Aided Design) at the high school level, I collaborated with like-minded educators and industry partners — including Maton and Cole Clark — to pioneer a standardised pathway for the craft. Together, we developed the curriculum for the Certificate IV in Musical Instrument Making and Repair.

For the past 15 years, I have had the privilege of teaching this rigorous course to adult students, passing on the precision, patience, and art of lutherie.

Global Insights & Scientific Precision

Teaching opened doors to a global community of master builders. My ongoing studies in the field led to exclusive invitations to Italy, where I attended prestigious guitar conferences and seminars in Cremona and Verona. Gaining hands-on, practical insights from some of the world's most esteemed builders fundamentally elevated my approach to construction.

Furthermore, I have extensively studied the physical and acoustic properties of tonewoods and guitar physics. This scientific approach ensures that every guitar isn't just visually stunning — it is mathematically and structurally engineered to produce the exact sound, resonance, and tactile feel a musician demands.

The Next Chapter

With retirement from formal teaching just around the corner, I am stepping out of the classroom and fully into the workshop. This next chapter is dedicated entirely to my true passion: pouring decades of experience, global knowledge, and raw dedication into building world-class, handcrafted guitars.
