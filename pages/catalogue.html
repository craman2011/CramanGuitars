Each guitar is a one-of-a-kind commission. The instruments below represent completed builds. Every specification can be adapted to your requirements.

Bespoke by Design

No two Craman guitars are identical. The instruments shown here are completed builds — each one a conversation made physical. They are presented as a reference point for what is possible, not as stock items awaiting shipment.

If a guitar shown here resonates with you, it is a starting point. We can build something close to it, or we can build something entirely your own.

Current Builds

Orchestra Model

Juliette

OM · Acoustic · One-Off Build

The flagship of the Craman catalogue. Juliette was conceived as a demonstration of what Huon pine can achieve in the hands of a builder who understands its physics. Balanced, articulate, and stunning under stage lighting — she plays as beautifully as she photographs.

  • BodyOrchestra Model (OM)
  • TopHuon Pine — Solid
  • Back & SidesFigured Maple
  • Neck1 piece Queensland Maple
  • FretboardMaple
  • Scale648 mm
  • FinishSatin Nitrocellulose
  • RosetteHand-inlaid purfling, Laser Craman signature
  • StatusCompleted — for reference
Orchestra Model

Romeo

OM · Acoustic · One-Off Build

Romeo was conceived as a companion to Huon pine OM Juliette. Built the following year after Juliette in Verona, Italy, Romeo was built within the walls the San Fermo Church, undoubtedly providing inspiration.
