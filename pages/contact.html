How a Commission Works

A typical commission begins with a conversation — in person, by phone, or by email — about how you play, what you play, and what you are looking for in an instrument. From there, Craig works through a specification with you covering body shape, scale length, tonewoods, bracing pattern, neck profile, nut width, finish, and any inlay or binding work.

Build times vary depending on complexity and current schedule. Craig will advise an estimated timeframe at the point of commission. A deposit secures your place in the queue and materials are sourced to your specification before work begins.

What to Include

If you already have ideas, feel free to share them — body style, tonewoods you are drawn to, playing style (fingerstyle, flatpick, classical), preferred scale length, or any guitars that have inspired you. If you are not sure, that is fine too. Craig will guide you through the options.
